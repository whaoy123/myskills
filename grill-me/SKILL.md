# Grill Me

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

## Rules of Engagement

1. **One Question at a Time**: Ask questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.
2. **Explore Environment First**: If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, codebase, tools, docs, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me with your recommendation and wait for my answer.
3. **Resolve Dependencies**: Walk down each branch of the design/decision tree, resolving prerequisites before moving to downstream details.
4. **Shared Understanding**: Do not act on or implement the plan until I explicitly confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
